Guess My Doodle · Removal and support

Guess My Doodle: Draw & Guess is developed by TRIPEX / Raven Ho, package com.tripex.guessmydoodle.

Start a request without reinstalling

Email tripexsupport@gmail.com about a privacy or removal request. Tell us what you want removed. Include the community support ID if available and say whether you still have access to the original installation. An ID helps us locate records; it does not by itself prove ownership. Do not email passwords, ID tokens or payment card details.

If the original app session is available

Version 0.1.1 and later: Settings → Remove shared contributions verifies control through the existing Firebase session and pauses new contribution work. It distinguishes an offline request waiting to send from one received by the server. This request covers attributable community drawings, answers and reports. Your local drawings and progress remain.

If you no longer have that session

You can still initiate a request by email. We review available information and use proportionate checks before removing records associated with someone else's public identifier. We explain what can be verified and the scope we can process; creating a new anonymous session does not prove control of an old one.

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 days and complete verified requests within 30 days. We explain verification steps, retained records and any additional time needed. You may request access, correction or deletion of other app-related data by email as well.

Scope and retained information

Turning participation off and uninstalling do not remove existing server records. Community removal concerns attributable developer-controlled contributions. Minimal restricted request/suppression records may be retained to track handling and prevent deleted uploads from returning. Prior exports, backups and third-party copies need separate review; copies downloaded by others may not be recallable.

The app does not offer a user-created login, username or cross-device account. Firebase provides a technical anonymous session for community actions. To request closure of that session or other associated data, state this in your email; the in-app community request is narrower. Other service data may use separate Firebase installation, advertising or RevenueCat identifiers. Ask support about those requests as well. Community removal is not a statement that all provider data or a full app account has been deleted. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription; subscription management remains in Google Play.
