Guess My Doodle · Privacy policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026.

App: Guess My Doodle: Draw & Guess · Package: com.tripex.guessmydoodle · Developer: TRIPEX / Raven Ho. Contact: tripexsupport@gmail.com.

Local practice and optional sharing

Your local drawings, labels, practice answers, response times, XP and streak are stored in the app's private storage. Local practice does not create community contribution jobs. Update checks, advertising and purchase services have separate data practices described below.

In version 0.1.1 and later, community participation requires an explicit choice in Settings. Earlier versions used an onboarding consent choice; update to access the revised local-practice and sharing controls. Firebase Authentication provides an anonymous account identifier that connects contributions and helps the app find your gallery. It is a pseudonymous identifier, not a promise that the service collects no identifying information. No name or email sign-in form is required.

When participation is enabled, community drawings are sent to Firestore with vector strokes, colors and widths, word/pack labels, creation time, uploader identifier and participation version/time. Community answers include the chosen word, correctness and submission time, linked to the anonymous identifier to measure agreement and prevent duplicate votes. Reports include a reporting identifier, reason and submission time. Old local sketches are uploaded only when you choose Share.

Community display and datasets

Shared drawings can be displayed to other players and may be included in an open CC0 dataset. CC0 permits reuse, including commercial use. It concerns copyright and does not remove applicable privacy rights. Avoid personal information in shared drawings. See CC0.

Participation and removal

Turning participation off stops new contribution jobs. Work already queued or in flight may finish. Opt-out does not by itself remove previously shared content. Upgrading does not automatically enroll an earlier player into the revised sharing flow.

Version 0.1.1 and later provides Settings → Remove shared contributions. It uses the existing community session to verify control of that identity, saves the request intent and pauses new contributions on the phone. Offline requests are shown as waiting to send; only server-confirmed requests are shown as received. Removal covers attributable community drawings, answers and reports. Local sketches and progress remain on the device.

Questions or requests can also be initiated through our removal and support page without reinstalling the app. A public support ID alone is not proof of ownership. If the original session is unavailable, we review what can be verified using proportionate checks. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

We aim to acknowledge privacy requests within 7 days and complete verified requests within 30 days. If a request needs more verification or additional time, we explain what is needed and the expected timing.

Storage and retention

Shared contributions are stored in Firebase and retained until a verified removal request is fulfilled; there is no automatic shared-contribution expiry. Uninstalling or clearing local storage does not by itself delete server records. We retain minimal restricted request/suppression records needed to prevent removed uploads from being reintroduced and to track request handling. We review developer-controlled copies identified during request handling and exclude accepted removals from future exports and restores. We keep minimal restricted status/suppression records while needed to honor the removal choice and prevent reintroduction; we review their necessity when handling a request. Copies already downloaded by other players or third parties may not be recallable.

A community removal request does not automatically delete information processed under separate installation, advertising or purchase identifiers, and it does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Contact support for other data requests; provider-specific handling and any legitimate retained records must be explained for the requested scope.

Update checks

Firebase Remote Config checks supported app versions independently of community participation. Firebase processes installation identifiers and technical information such as country/language codes, time zone, operating-system/platform version and app information. Firebase Authentication also processes IP addresses and technical request information when community sign-in is used. See Firebase disclosures.

Advertising and purchases

Google Mobile Ads provides advertising and may process advertising/device identifiers, interactions, diagnostics and IP-derived approximate location. Version 0.1.1 and later checks Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads and offers an advertising privacy-options control where Google requires it. The revised app applies protective under-age-of-consent treatment, requests non-personalized ads and limits ad content to a teen rating or below for all users because it does not collect age. Research participation does not control these services. See Google Mobile Ads disclosures.

RevenueCat and Google Play process Pro purchases, including purchase history and associated identifiers. RevenueCat's generated App User ID is separate from the Firebase community identifier. Changing research preferences or requesting community removal does not cancel a subscription. See RevenueCat disclosures.

Audience, updates and contact

The app's declared audience is ages 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support so the request can be reviewed. We update this policy when approved data practices change. Questions and privacy requests: tripexsupport@gmail.com.
